# Credits

Cape Sorrow carries no third-party work of its own yet. What it ships of other people's comes with the engine it is built on: models, textures and sounds published on Sketchfab, Poly Haven, ambientCG and Freesound, and the libraries the engine draws with. The engine credits each of them in its own `CREDITS.md`, and the game's Credits screen shows that list and this one as one. A work of someone else's that the game itself carries is listed below, one section to a work, when there is one.
