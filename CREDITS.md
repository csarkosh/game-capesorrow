# Credits

The works of others that Cape Sorrow itself carries: the town's texture sets under `client/assets/textures/`, which `tools/textures/build.py` resizes and re-encodes (the paintable ones are made into neutral detail for the game's paint colours to multiply). One section to a work, each under its licence, in the shape the Credits screen reads (`client/src/game/credits.ts`). What the game ships of other people's besides comes with the engine it is built on: models, textures and sounds published on Sketchfab, Poly Haven, ambientCG and Freesound, and the libraries the engine draws with. The engine credits each of them in its own `CREDITS.md`, and the game's Credits screen shows that list and this one as one. The asphalt shingle layer is original work, made by the build script, and is not listed.
